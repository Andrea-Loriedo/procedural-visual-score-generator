Visual Score Key

Free interpretation is encouraged; however, here is one way to read the generated score:
  • Circle / Sphere: Sustained tone or drone
  • Square / Box: Chord or harmonic block
  • Triangle / Pyramid: Percussive or staccato event
  • Color (Hue): Instrument or timbre (assign colors to instrument families)
  • Brightness: Dynamic (brighter = louder)
  • Saturation: Expressiveness (more saturated = more expressive)
  • Horizontal (X): Time (left = earlier, right = later)
  • Vertical (Y): Pitch (higher = higher pitch, lower = lower pitch)
  • Depth (Z, 3D): Register or spatial placement
  • Size: Larger = longer duration or more important; Smaller = shorter or less prominent
  • Line Style:
    • Straight: Steady, continuous sound or phrase
    • Curved: Melodic contour or expressive gesture
    • Zigzag: Rhythmic pattern or syncopation
    • Wave: Oscillation, trill, or repeated figure
